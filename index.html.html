CAREER EXPLORATION

PM or PjM — what’s the difference?

Job titles can be misleading. Focus on what the role manages and what outcomes it owns.

🧭
PM · Product Manager

What should we build, and why?

PMs focus on the product: understanding users and markets, shaping product direction, prioritizing requirements, and measuring product value.

Typical responsibilities

User researchProduct roadmapPrioritizationProduct outcomes

🗂️
PjM · Project Manager

How do we deliver the agreed work?

PjMs focus on project delivery: planning schedules, coordinating resources, tracking milestones, and managing risks within agreed constraints.

Typical responsibilities

Project planningMilestonesRisk managementResource coordination
